MIT Mysuru
Digital Campus GIS.

A campus mapping and geospatial documentation concept exploring how physical features can be organised into a structured digital GIS project.

CREATOR: YASHAVANTH ANGUIDANCE: MANJUNATH RNAREA: GIS & DIGITAL MAPPING

Project overview

The MIT Mysuru Digital Campus GIS project focuses on representing campus features as geographic information. The concept is to organise mapped features and their descriptive attributes in a way that can support campus understanding and future information management.

Project objectives

  • Organise selected campus features into clear spatial layers.
  • Represent buildings, internal roads and other approved campus assets.
  • Maintain descriptive attributes alongside mapped geometry.
  • Prepare documentation that explains the data structure and workflow.

Potential data layers

  • Campus boundary: approved campus extent and reference boundary.
  • Buildings: building footprints and verified names or identifiers.
  • Roads and pathways: internal movement routes.
  • Facilities: selected public-facing facilities and landmarks.
  • Utilities: only where information is authorised for collection and publication.
A campus GIS must use authorised source information. Exact coordinates, utility layouts and sensitive infrastructure details should not be published without permission.

Tools and methods

The project is intended to use GIS concepts and tools such as QGIS, spatial layers, attribute tables and appropriate geographic data formats. The specific software versions, coordinate reference system, survey methods and data sources should be recorded in the final technical report.
